    What this one is for

    Turning epistemics into something you can look at. Most food graphs present every connection with the same confidence, which is the reason bad claims propagate. Here the dashed red edges are refusals — unevidenced_link, links other people assert that this dataset declines to draw — and they stay visible right to the end of the sweep.

    The uncomfortable one

    Sweep to the far right and look for the Teochew node. The claim that Teochews dominate Penang's street food trade is the load-bearing premise of a great deal of writing about this island, including Michelin's, and no scholarly source establishes it.
