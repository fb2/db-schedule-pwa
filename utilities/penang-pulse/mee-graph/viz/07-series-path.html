The Series Path

Every bowl so far, in the order it was eaten, drawn as a route across the cultural map rather than a list. Faded dots are what is still on the checklist. Where the trail keeps returning is where the eating has been concentrated — and the empty quarters are the argument for the next few episodes.

Episode

Dots sit near whichever community, noodle or venue they belong to, so the same set of episodes rearranges completely when you change the grouping. Click any dot for the episode.

What this one is for

Turning a checklist into a map of coverage. Grouped by community it is immediately obvious that the run so far is Hokkien-heavy with a strong Teochew second — which is roughly what the island's demography predicts. Episode 16, the Jones Road mee sotong, finally opened the Indian-Muslim thread; the thin spots now are Cantonese (one bowl), the Malay and Javanese side, and the Siamese substrate.

Editorially useful

Switch to By venue and the clustering at Restoran 77, 33 Best Food Hub and Hock Beng 88 is a fair reminder that a series can accidentally become a review of three food courts.
