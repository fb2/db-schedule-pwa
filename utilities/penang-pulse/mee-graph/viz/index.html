How to read the set

Views 01, 02 and 04 are the ones a reader would use to answer a question. View 03 is the one that makes an argument. Views 05 and 06 are editorial — they are about what we do not know. View 07 is the series' own progress, and the most likely candidate for a permanent home on the series index page.

Embedding

Each file is standalone and reads graph-data.js, so any of them drops into an <iframe> or gets inlined into a guide template. d3 is vendored as d3.v7.min.js. The public Mee-Search landing lives under the series hub at /guides/series/mee-myself-and-i/mee-search/. See README.md in this folder.
