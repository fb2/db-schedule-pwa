The Culture Web

Communities hold their ground; dishes drift between them. Tap a community to pull its dishes into orbit — tap a second and any dish they share is pulled taut between the two. That tension is the whole argument: most Penang bowls have more than one parent.

Gravity

Drag any node to reposition it. Solid lines are cooked-by; dashed lines are somebody's contested claim. Node size is how connected the thing is, not how good it tastes.

What this one is for

Answering “whose dish is this, really?” without pretending there is one answer. The graph keeps carried_by (whose kitchen) apart from attributed_to (somebody's claim), so a dish like mee rebus can visibly hang between the Javanese and Tamil-Muslim nodes instead of being filed under one of them.
