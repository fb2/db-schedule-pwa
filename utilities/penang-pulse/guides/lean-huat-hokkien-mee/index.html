Lean Huat Hokkien Mee

First stop in Mee Myself and I — rich prawn-pork broth at Swee Kong Kopitiam, next to Pulau Tikus Market.

Neighbourhood · Pulau Tikus

Penang’s Hokkien mee (prawn mee) is a broth game: pork bones, dried shrimp, and time. Lean Huat is the bowl I keep recommending from Pulau Tikus — close enough to Common Ground that it works as a morning mission before the queue empties the pot.

Spot

Lean Huat Hokkien Mee

Swee Kong Kopitiam, Jalan Burma (opposite / next to Pulau Tikus Market)

Open in Google Maps

Tasting notes

  • Broth — deep prawn-pork, not watery; the kind that coats the spoon.
  • Noodles — yellow mee with bee hoon; ask for mixed if you want both textures.
  • Toppings — prawns, sliced pork, egg; keep expectations realistic for a hawker stall, not a tasting menu.
  • Timing — go early (before 8–9am). It sells out; afternoon visits are a gamble.
