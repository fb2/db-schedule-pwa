Morning at Pulau Tikus Market

A short photo walk through wet market aisles, kopitiam tables, and the lunch rush — placeholder images for layout review.

Photo article · ~5 min read

Arrive before 9am if you want the full wet-market energy: produce stacked high, fish on ice, and noodle stalls already working through the first queue of the day.

The hawker section sits close to the wet market. Follow the steam: Hokkien mee and curry mee stalls are the usual draw for a late breakfast that doubles as lunch.
