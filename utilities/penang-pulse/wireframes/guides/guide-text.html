Hawker lunch near Common Ground

Walkable noodle picks in Pulau Tikus — for coworking days when you want real hawker food without a long ride.

Neighbourhood · Pulau Tikus / Jalan Burma

Common Ground sits in the heart of Pulau Tikus (Jalan Moulmein / Jalan Burma), so you have excellent walkable or very short-ride access to strong noodle options for lunch. Focus here is noodle soups near the office.

Top picks near Common Ground

  • Hokkien mee (prawn mee) at Lean Huat Hokkien Mee (Swee Kong Kopitiam, Jalan Burma, opposite / next to Pulau Tikus Market). One of the most praised in Penang — rich prawn-pork broth, yellow noodles and bee hoon, prawns, pork, egg. Go early (before 8–9am); it often sells out.
  • Curry mee at Sister’s Curry Mee (Jalan Burma, opposite Pulau Tikus Market). Fragrant coconut-spicy broth; often sells out by noon. Very convenient from Common Ground.
  • Wantan mee at Wantan Mee House (Jalan Burma). Solid neighbourhood spot for dry or soup with good wontons — reliable for a quick lunch.
  • Koay teow th’ng (flat rice noodle soup) in the Pulau Tikus Market area or nearby kopitiams. Light clear broth with fish balls or minced pork — a refreshing, lighter option.
  • Lam mee at some market / kopitiam stalls (Nyonya-style with starchy prawn gravy when you find it).

Practical tips

  • Pulau Tikus Market hawker area is a short walk and the best bet for morning-to-lunch noodle soups.
  • Swee Kong Kopitiam and nearby spots (e.g. Foodyard 77, Sin Hup Aun) cluster several noodle stalls in one place.
  • Inside Common Ground, The Warung covers rice bowls and café fare — for authentic hawker noodles, step out.
  • Best times: arrive before noon to beat crowds and sell-outs. Many stalls are cash-only.
