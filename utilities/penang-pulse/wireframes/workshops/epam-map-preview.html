Getting around · practical tips

Wireframe of the logistics block for the production page — keep it short and useful, not a travel essay.

  • Install Grab Penang’s convenient rideshare. Set it up on your phone before you land (or right after).
  • Airport pickup There’s a Grab pickup zone outside the Arrivals hall at Penang International (PEN).
  • Airport → hotel Grab to Gurney Bay Hotel / PARKROYAL is usually about 30–45 minutes; allow ~45–60 minutes in rush-hour traffic.
  • Carry some RM cash Street vendors and many hawkers still prefer cash — keep small notes handy.
  • Dress for the heat Warm, humid weather year-round. Light clothes, and expect AC indoors.
